# Responsible AI & Safety Guidelines

VisionIQ adheres to Microsoft Responsible AI principles:

## 1. Fairness & Inclusiveness
- Ensure vision recognition algorithms perform reliably across diverse image qualities, cultural contexts, and product categories.

## 2. Reliability & Safety
- Guard against hallucinations by enforcing strict grounding in Azure AI Search data.
- Validate media inputs against harmful content using Azure AI Content Safety filters.

## 3. Privacy & Security
- Never store sensitive or personally identifiable visual media without consent.
- Keep all API credentials and connection strings in environment variables (never committed).

## 4. Transparency & Accountability
- Provide clear visual indicators when answers are generated by AI.
- Supply source citations and confidence metrics for identified products and video segments.
