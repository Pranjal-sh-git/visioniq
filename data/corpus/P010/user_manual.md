- Regular inspection: Periodically check fasteners for tightness and examine the mesh and foam for wear.
- Cleaning mesh: Vacuum mesh to remove dust. For spot cleaning, use a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water; blot—do not rub aggressively.
- Cleaning foam and frame: Wipe molded foam seat and nylon frame with a damp cloth and mild detergent. Rinse cloth and wipe dry.

- Drying: Allow any cleaned surfaces to air-dry fully before use.

## 5. Basic Troubleshooting
- Chair wobbles or feels unstable:
  - Check that the gas lift and base are fully seated and that casters are fully inserted.
  - Ensure all screws and bolts are tightened evenly.

- Recline will not lock:
  - Return the seat to an upright position, operate the tilt control fully, and try again. If necessary, tighten any accessible tilt mechanism fasteners.

- Lumbar support feels loose or too stiff:
  - Adjust the lumbar tension according to personal comfort. If adjustments have no effect, inspect the lumbar mechanism for obstruction or misalignment.

- Squeaks or creaks:
  - Identify the area of noise, then tighten adjacent fasteners and apply a small amount of lubricant to moving metal-on-metal contacts only (avoid mesh and foam).

- Damage or defects:
  - If you find material defects or component failure, please refer to the 2-year warranty and contact Autonomous customer support for repair or replacement options.
