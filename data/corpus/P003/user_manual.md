## 5. Basic Troubleshooting
No sound or intermittent audio
- Ensure the headphones are connected via Bluetooth 5.0 to the source device.
- Verify volume on both the AirPods Max (Digital Crown) and the source device is up.
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting the Bluetooth connection.

Active Noise Cancellation not working as expected
- Confirm the device is set to Active Noise Cancellation and not Transparency mode via your source device’s audio settings.
- Ensure ear cushions are seated correctly and create an adequate seal.

Short battery life
- Confirm the headphones were placed in the Smart Case when idle to enter ultra-low-power mode.
- Reduce excessive use of high-volume playback and noise-intensive modes, and ensure the headphones are charged before long sessions.

Pairing fails
