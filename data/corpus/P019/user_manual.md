## 2. Setup & Initial Configuration

Before first use
1. Inspect: Remove shoes from the box and inspect for any shipping damage. Verify both left and right shoes are present and the Quicklace system functions by pulling the lace to tighten and releasing the toggle to lock.
2. Fit check: Put each shoe on and ensure the EndoFit internal sleeve comfortably hugs your foot. Walk a few steps on a flat surface while standing to confirm there are no pressure points. Adjust the Quicklace until fit feels secure but not constrictive.
3. Break‑in: Wear the shoes on short, easy runs or walks on forgiving surfaces for the first outings to allow the materials and your feet to adapt. If you feel persistent discomfort, re-check lacing, socks, and correct size before longer or more technical use.

Quicklace use
- To tighten: pull the Quicklace once until the desired fit is reached.
- To secure: slide the lace toggle down to lock the lace in place.
- To loosen: lift the toggle or release the lace toggle and gently pull the lace back through to create more slack.

Notes
- The Sensifit and EndoFit systems are designed to cradle and hug the foot respectively—use lacing adjustments to fine-tune midfoot and forefoot fit.
- The Profeel Film and chassis are intended to add protection and stability on trail terrain; choose terrain appropriate to your experience and the shoe’s design.

## 4. Maintenance & Cleaning Instructions

Cleaning
- Remove loose dirt by gently tapping soles together or using a soft brush.
- Hand wash with lukewarm water and a mild soap. Use a soft brush or cloth to clean upper, midsole, and outsole.
- Rinse thoroughly to remove soap residue.

Drying and care
- Air dry at room temperature away from direct heat or sunlight. Do not tumble dry or place near heaters.
- Avoid machine washing and drying to preserve welded TPU film and integrated features.
- If laces or toggles become clogged with debris, rinse and allow to dry before next use.

Storage
- Store in a cool, dry place away from prolonged direct sunlight. Allow shoes to dry completely before storing to prevent odor or material degradation.

## 5. Basic Troubleshooting

Quicklace will not tighten
- Check for debris in the lace channel or toggle. Rinse and dry the toggle and lace.
- Ensure lace is not frayed or broken. If damaged, contact Salomon for replacement options.

Persistent discomfort or pressure points
- Re-check lacing tension and adjust Quicklace to redistribute pressure.
- Confirm correct shoe size. If discomfort continues, consider alternate sock thickness or consult a retailer for fit advice.

Reduced traction on outsole
- Inspect Mud Contagrip deep chevron lugs for packed mud or stones. Clean lugs with a brush and rinse.
- If lugs show severe wear or damage, consult an authorized service provider for assessment.

Damage to upper or chassis
- Small scuffs on TPU film are normal from trail use; deep cuts or separation of welded mesh should be inspected by a repair professional or returned to Salomon where applicable.
