# Salomon XT-6 - Usage Guide

## 1. Ergonomics & Ideal Fit / Placement
- Designed for trail running and technical outdoor wear, the Salomon XT-6 combines an EndoFit internal fit sleeve and Sensifit wrap to create a close, secure fit around the midfoot and ankle. Expect a snug cradle from the midsole into the lacing area.
- Upper construction uses abrasion-resistant TPU film and mesh with a welded TPU film layer on the mesh. This provides structure around the foot while remaining breathable where mesh is exposed.
- The Quicklace one-pull tightening system allows fast, single-action cinching. Use small adjustments after lacing to balance comfort at the forefoot and lockdown at the midfoot.
- The Agile Chassis System (ACS) with EVA cushioning integrates stability and cushioning. The chassis is intended to sit under the foot to guide footstrike and control motion on uneven terrain.
- The Mud Contagrip outsole with deep chevron lugs offers aggressive traction; place weight and foot strikes mindful of uneven ground to take advantage of the lug geometry.
- Weight reference: 365g (Size 9). Heaviness and feel will vary by size; consider this when choosing based on preferred carry weight.

## 2. Optimal Performance & Environmental Recommendations
- Best performance on trails, muddier surfaces, and technical downhill sections where the Mud Contagrip outsole and chevron lugs can bite and shed mud.
- The Profeel Film layer helps protect the foot from sharp terrain and debris—avoid exposing the upper to prolonged abrasion against very sharp rock edges to preserve the protective layer.
- For wet conditions, use natural gait and footing to allow the chevron lugs to evacuate mud and maintain traction; avoid sustained use on highly polished rock without testing traction first.
- The welded TPU film and mesh upper balances durability and airflow; in very hot climates, monitor breathability and allow cooling breaks as necessary.
- Avoid prolonged submersion in salt water or chemically treated pools; rinse with fresh water after incidental exposure.

## 3. Best Practices for Product Longevity
- Cleaning: After use, remove excess mud by tapping soles together or brushing the outsole. Wipe the upper with a soft brush or cloth and mild soap; rinse with fresh water. Loosen the Quicklace before cleaning to access the tongue and entry.
- Drying: Air-dry at room temperature away from direct heat sources and direct sunlight to prevent material deformation. Do not use tumble dryers or high-temperature heat.
- Storage: Store in a cool, dry place. Allow shoes to dry completely before long-term storage to avoid odors and material breakdown.
- Inspections: Periodically check the welded TPU film and mesh upper, the Profeel Film area, and the outsole lugs for signs of excessive wear or damage. Address abrasive wear early to extend usable life.
- Repairs: For lace hardware or minor upper damage, seek professional cobbler service or manufacturer repair options rather than DIY adhesives that may compromise materials.

## 4. Recommended Use Cases & Scenarios
- Trail running on mixed technical terrain where stability and protective layering are needed.
- Long approaches and gear-forward “gorpcore” urban/outdoor hybrid activities that benefit from durable uppers and stable chassis.
- Technical downhill segments where chassis stability and chevron lug geometry aid confident footing.
- Day hikes and fastpacking where a balance of protection, traction, and a secure fit are required.
