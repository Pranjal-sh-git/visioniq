# Apple Watch Ultra 2 - Warranty

Note: This is an illustrative sample document, not the manufacturer's official warranty policy.

## 1. Warranty Coverage & Duration
Apple provides a limited warranty for the Apple Watch Ultra 2 that covers defects in materials and workmanship. The exact duration of the limited warranty varies by country or region and may be affected by local consumer protection laws. To determine the specific warranty period that applies to your purchase, review the warranty information included with your product, consult your proof of purchase, or contact Apple Support or an Apple Authorized Service Provider.

Important: statutory rights under local law (for example, consumer protection laws) may apply in addition to, or instead of, the limited warranty described here.

## 2. What Is Covered
The limited warranty covers, during the applicable warranty period, defects in materials and workmanship in the Apple Watch Ultra 2. Examples of covered items and issues include:
- Defects in the 49mm Titanium case that result from manufacturing faults.
- Manufacturing defects affecting the Always-On Retina OLED display, including failures preventing normal operation of the display rated at 3000 nits peak brightness.
- Faults in the internal electronics and sensors, including the S9 SiP and its gesture functionality, ECG, Blood Oxygen, and temperature sensing, when those faults are due to manufacturing defects.
- Failures of the precision dual-frequency GPS (L1 and L5) hardware when caused by defects in materials or workmanship.
- Issues that prevent operation of features described in the product specifications and feature list supplied with the product, including the Customizable Action button, Emergency Siren, and the modular Ultra watch face metrics, when such issues are due to manufacturing defects.

Coverage is limited to repair, replacement with a new or refurbished equivalent device, or refund at Apple's discretion and in accordance with local law and Apple policy.

## 3. What Is Excluded
The limited warranty does not cover damage or malfunction resulting from:
- Misuse, accident, abuse, neglect, unauthorized immersion, or exposure beyond the product’s rated environmental specifications.
- Normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage (scratches, dents, or changes to finish of the titanium case), or deterioration resulting from use over time.
- Unauthorized modifications, third-party repairs, or the installation of non-Apple parts and accessories.
- Damage from third-party services, software, or accessories not supplied or approved by Apple.
- Loss, theft, or inability to operate the device because of lost passwords or Activation Lock.

Examples of exclusions include but are not limited to broken display due to impact, water damage from immersion beyond 100m water resistance or outside EN13319 dive certification limits, and problems caused by third-party straps, chargers, or adapters.
