# Humanscale Freedom - Usage Guide

## 1. Ergonomics & Ideal Fit / Placement
- Goal: let the chair support a natural, balanced posture so your muscles remain relaxed during work.
- Sit fully into the seat so your back contacts the contoured cushions. The pivoting backrest adapts to your lumbar curve; allow it to settle as you shift and recline.
- The weight-sensitive counter-balance recline eliminates manual tension knobs. To find a neutral balance position, sit naturally and allow the recline to respond to your body weight — avoid forcing the chair into an extreme position.
- Use the synchronous armrests to support your forearms while typing and when transitioning between tasks. Position your monitor and desk so your elbows are close to a natural right angle; armrests should support the forearms without lifting the shoulders.
- The dynamic headrest moves with the recline to maintain head support during forward and reclined tasks — use it to reduce neck strain when reclining.
- Placement: position the chair on a stable, level surface with adequate clearance behind and to the sides to allow full recline and armrest movement.

- Materials: the chair combines die-cast aluminum with Corde 4-way stretch fabric. These materials perform best in typical indoor office or home environments.
- Environmental best practices: avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight which can accelerate fabric fading and material aging. Keep the chair in a dry, well-ventilated area and avoid placing it in consistently damp or corrosive environments.
- Flooring: use on stable, flat floors. If using casters on hard floors, consider a protective mat to reduce wear and maintain smooth movement.
