## 2. Setup & Initial Configuration
Before first use, inspect the shoes for any visible defects. Follow these steps to ensure a proper initial fit and setup.

1. Try the shoes on with the socks you normally run in. Loosen the laces fully, slide your foot in until your heel rests in the Fitcounter heel cup, then tighten laces from the bottom up for an even snug fit.
2. Stand and walk for a few minutes on a flat surface to confirm fit. The PRIMEKNIT+ upper is engineered to provide a sock-like feel; expect a secure but comfortable wrap. If you feel pressure points, re-lace to redistribute tension or try an alternate lacing pattern.
3. Take a short test run (easy pace, short distance) on a safe surface. Confirm the Linear Energy Push (LEP) responsiveness and Continental outsole traction on wet and dry surfaces before longer runs.

Notes

Fit issues
- Tight or painful spots: Re-lace using a different pattern, loosen the offending eyelets, or use thinner socks.

Comfort or break-in discomfort
- Mild discomfort during early use: Reduce distance and increase gradually. If pain persists, discontinue use and consult a specialist.

Traction or outsole problems

Squeaks or noise
