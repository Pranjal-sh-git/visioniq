## Q: What are typical daily usage adjustments and tips for comfort?
A: Start with a neutral seated position and then fine-tune:

- Tilt and tilt limiter: Use the Harmonic 2 tilt mechanism to recline smoothly; engage the tilt limiter to stop the recline at a comfortable angle for tasks or breaks.
- Forward tilt: If you need a more engaged forward seating posture (for typing or focused work), engage forward tilt and re-check sacral support and arm position.
