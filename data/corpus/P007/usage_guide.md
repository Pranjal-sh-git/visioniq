# Herman Miller Aeron Chair - Usage Guide

Brief
This guide explains how to use and care for the Herman Miller Aeron Chair. It references the chair’s material and adjustment features and provides practical, safety-conscious guidance to optimize ergonomics, performance, and longevity. Refer to your local Herman Miller service or dealer for replacement parts and certified repairs.

## 1. Ergonomics & Ideal Fit / Placement
- Purpose: The Aeron is an ergonomic icon engineered to support the human body. It uses an 8Z Pellicle elastomeric mesh with pressure distribution zonal mesh and PostureFit SL sacral support to promote healthy spinal alignment.
- Fit: The Aeron is available in sizes A, B, and C. Select the size that best matches your body proportions and workspace ergonomics.
- Seat and posture setup (recommended steps):
  1. Sit back so your sacrum contacts the PostureFit SL support to stabilize the lower spine.
  2. Adjust the tilt using the Harmonic 2 tilt mechanism and tilt limiter so your torso can recline with support; use forward tilt when you need a slight forward angle for focused tasks.
  3. Set arm height and width with the fully adjustable arms so forearms are parallel to the desk surface and shoulders remain relaxed.
  4. Ensure your feet rest flat on the floor or a footrest; knees should be roughly level with hips.
- Placement: Position the chair on a stable, level floor surface close to the work surface so you do not overreach. Keep the chair in a space that allows free movement of the tilt mechanism and arms.

## 2. Optimal Performance & Environmental Recommendations
- Materials and breathability: The 8Z Pellicle mesh provides zonal pressure distribution and breathability. Avoid placing the chair where extended direct exposure to intense sunlight may accelerate material aging.
- Environmental certifications: The product is Cradle to Cradle V3 Certified Silver and uses a recycled aluminum base, which supports sustainable use and end-of-life considerations.
- Indoor use: Use the chair primarily indoors in office and home-office environments. Keep it away from prolonged exposure to moisture and corrosive chemicals.
- Temperature: Normal indoor room temperatures are appropriate. Sudden or extreme temperature swings can affect mechanical components over time.

## 3. Best Practices for Product Longevity
- Cleaning: Wipe the mesh and frame with a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water. Do not use abrasive cleaners, bleach, or solvents on the mesh or finish.
- Periodic checks: Inspect fasteners and adjustment controls periodically for proper operation. If a component feels loose, stop using that adjustment until it is inspected or serviced.
- Avoid misuse: Do not stand on the chair, use it as a step ladder, or subject it to impacts beyond normal seating use.
- Service and warranty: The chair weight is 18.6 kg and it carries a 12 years warranty. Keep purchase documentation and contact Herman Miller or your authorized dealer for warranty service and approved part replacements.

## 4. Recommended Use Cases & Scenarios

- Collaborative spaces and home offices: Suitable for shared environments where durable, breathable seating is desirable.
- Users requiring spinal support: PostureFit SL and zonal mesh are intended to reduce pressure points and support sacral alignment.
- Load limits: Observe the maximum load of 159 kg (350 lbs). Do not exceed this limit.
