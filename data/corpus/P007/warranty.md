Coverage is provided to the original purchaser and is subject to the terms and exclusions described below.

During the 12-year warranty period Herman Miller will, at its discretion, repair or replace components that are found to be defective in materials or workmanship. Covered components include, but are not limited to:
- Structural components and frame, including the recycled aluminum base listed in the product specifications.
- The 8Z Pellicle elastomeric mesh suspension membrane and related attachment points.
- Adjustment and support systems such as PostureFit SL, Harmonic 2 tilt mechanism, tilt limiter, forward tilt, and fully adjustable arms.
- Mechanical failures that prevent the chair from performing as intended when used within its specified limits (the product is specified to accommodate a maximum load of 159 kg (350 lbs)).

- Cosmetic damage: scratches, dents, stains, or other surface blemishes that do not affect function.
- Misuse, abuse, or neglect: damage resulting from improper use, excessive or abusive loading beyond the published maximum load of 159 kg (350 lbs), intentional damage, or use inconsistent with the product’s intended function.

This list is representative but not exhaustive. Validity of a claim will be determined by inspection and at Herman Miller’s discretion.
