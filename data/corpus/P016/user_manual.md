## 5. Basic Troubleshooting
Problem: Shoes feel tight or cause pressure points
- Solution: Re-lace using a different pattern, loosen top eyelets, or allow additional break-in time. If persistent, consider trying a different size or width.

Problem: Heel slips during activity
- Solution: Use a heel-lock lacing technique (runner’s loop) and ensure the tongue is centered. Check that the heel tab is not folded.

Problem: Slippery or excessive outsole wear
- Solution: Inspect the Ndurance rubber outsole for uneven wear. Rotate use with another pair of shoes and avoid abrasive surfaces when possible. If tread is worn smooth, consider replacement.

Problem: Suede scuffs or stains

Problem: Midsole feels compressed or less responsive
- Solution: Midsole materials such as FuelCell foam and ENCAP may compress over long-term use. If cushioning performance is noticeably degraded, consult New Balance customer support or consider replacing the shoes.
