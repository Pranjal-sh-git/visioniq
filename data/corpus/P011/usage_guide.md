# IKEA Markus - Usage Guide

This guide covers safe use, placement, care, and best practices for the IKEA Markus high-back mesh desk chair. Refer to your local IKEA documentation and the included assembly sheet for parts and assembly verification.

## 1. Ergonomics & Ideal Fit / Placement
- Purpose: The high backrest provides support for the neck and upper spine and the built-in lumbar cushion supports the lower back. The breathable polyester mesh helps keep the back cool during extended sitting.
- Adjustments available: Seat height adjustment and synchronized tilt tension and lock. Use the seat height control to set your hips slightly above or level with your knees for a neutral posture. Use the synchronized tilt tension to control how easily the chair reclines and the tilt lock to hold a preferred reclined angle.
- Fit and clearance: Ensure the chair has enough clearance behind and to the sides to allow tilt and movement without obstruction. Keep a clear area behind the chair and under your desk so the steel star base and castors can move freely.
- Load limit: Do not exceed the maximum load of 110 kg (242 lbs). This value is the design limit for safe use.

## 2. Optimal Performance & Environmental Recommendations
- Materials: The chair uses polyester mesh, polyurethane foam, and a steel star base. These materials perform best in temperate indoor environments.
- Floor surface: Pressure-sensitive safety brake castors are included to reduce unintended rolling. They are designed with safety in mind; performance is best on smooth, level indoor floors. Use a suitable floor protector or mat if you have delicate flooring.
- Temperature and humidity: Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight or very humid conditions, which can accelerate wear of mesh and foam materials.
- Moving the chair: The product weight is 20.8 kg. When relocating, lift carefully—use proper lifting technique or assistance to avoid strain. Avoid dragging the chair across rough surfaces to protect the castors and base.

## 3. Best Practices for Product Longevity
- Regular inspection: Periodically check screws, castors, and the star base for tightness, wear, or damage. Tighten loose fasteners following the original assembly instructions.
- Cleaning:
  - Mesh: Vacuum gently to remove dust. Wipe with a soft cloth dampened with mild soap and water; allow to air dry.
  - Foam and upholstery: Blot spills promptly with a clean cloth. Avoid saturating the foam. Follow mild soap and water cleaning; do not use bleach or strong solvents.
  - Metal base: Wipe with a damp cloth and dry to prevent corrosion.
- Castors: Keep castors free of hair and debris. Remove accumulated material from the wheels to maintain smooth rolling and braking action.
- Storage: Store indoors in a dry area if the chair will not be used for an extended period. Avoid stacking heavy objects on the chair.

- Meeting rooms or collaborative spaces: Use where balanced support and a professional appearance are desired.
