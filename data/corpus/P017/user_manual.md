- Regular cleaning
  - Remove loose dirt by brushing with a soft brush. Wipe the upper (recycled polyester engineered mesh) with a damp cloth and mild soap if needed. Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach.

  - Air dry at room temperature away from direct heat sources and sunlight. Do not use a dryer or direct heating, which can damage the Helion superfoam and Speedboard.

- Sockliner and interior
  - The soft sockliner and upper provide step-in comfort; wipe lightly with a damp cloth. If odor develops, allow shoes to air out thoroughly between uses and alternate pairs when possible.

## 5. Basic Troubleshooting
Common issues and practical fixes.

- Pinching or hot spots
  - Adjust lacing to redistribute pressure. Try different sock thicknesses. If persistent, consider a different lacing pattern or consult a fitting specialist.

- Heel slip
  - Tighten the upper collar and midfoot lacing. Ensure you are using the correct size; if heel slip continues, contact your retailer.

- Excessive wear or loss of cushioning
  - Inspect areas of high wear on the midsole and outsole. If cushioning feels significantly reduced, consider replacing the shoes; continued use may affect comfort and performance.

- Odor
